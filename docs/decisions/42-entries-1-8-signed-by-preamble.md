# Decision #42

[← decision log](../DECISIONS.md) · status: active

**Entries 1–8 are signed by the preamble; no signature is invented.** 26 of the 34 original entries carry a `*Signed …*` line; all eight without one are #1–#8,
whose only signature is the file header `# Design decisions (signed 2026-10-29)`. Cutting the
log by “find the signature line” would have left them looking like unsigned drafts, or
worse, tempted someone to backfill dates. Decision: their pages state
“signed by the log preamble, no per-entry signature line”, and the index marks them
`preamble`. Cost: for #1–#8 a reader cannot see who signed per entry — that is the record,
not a formatting choice.
